# FFXI Zone Region Reference

Generated from LandSandBoat current zone/region enums plus DATura zone display names. `official_region_*` is the server/game region returned by `GetCurrentRegion`; `lore_region` keeps transport, internal, private, and newer uncategorized zones from being forced into a conquest region.

Sources: LandSandBoat `src/map/zone.h`, `scripts/enum/region.lua`, `src/map/utils/zoneutils.cpp`; DATura `DATura/Zone_DATs_Table.csv`.
